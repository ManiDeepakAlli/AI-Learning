## COMMON QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

**Q: Do I need a GPU?**
A: Helpful but not essential. Use free GPU on Google Colab for training. For production, you'll need cloud GPU.

**Q: Should I learn TensorFlow or PyTorch?**
A: Focus on PyTorch (industry standard). Learn TensorFlow/Keras as secondary.

**Q: How much math do I really need?**
A: Conceptual understanding > deep proofs. You need to know what gradient descent does, not derive it.

**Q: Should I do a master's degree?**
A: No. 9 months of focused learning > 2 years of part-time study. Your experience matters more.

**Q: How do I know if I'm on track?**
A: After Week 14, you should feel comfortable explaining deep learning. After Week 22, you should feel comfortable building with LLMs. After Week 26, you should feel ready for interviews.

**Q: What if I fall behind?**
A: It's OK. Adjust timeline but keep consistent. Better to spend 12 months deeply understanding than 6 months surface-level.

**Q: How do I stay current with AI?**
A: Read papers (arxiv), follow key researchers (Yann LeCun, Andrej Karpathy, etc.), use AI communities (r/MachineLearning, Hugging Face forums).
